HelaService — Account & Data Deletion

Instructions on how to delete your account and associated personal data across HelaService in Sri Lanka.

English

Request Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time either directly from the HelaService mobile app or by contacting our support team.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)
  1. Open the HelaService mobile app.
  2. Navigate to Account / Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account Permanently.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your data will be wiped immediately.
Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, email our privacy desk with your registered phone number or email:

Email: support@helaservice.lk

Your request will be processed within 48 hours, and all verification documents, location records, and credentials will be purged.

Data That Is Permanently Deleted:

  • Profile information: Name, phone number, email address, profile photo.
  • Artisan verification records: National ID (NIC) numbers, NIC front/back photos, licenses, and certificates.
  • Live locations, saved addresses, device notification tokens (FCM), and private credentials.
  • Historical logs: Your name in completed orders is anonymized to "Deleted user" and contact numbers are permanently removed.
සිංහල (Sinhala)

ගිණුම සහ පුද්ගලික දත්ත මකා දැමීම

ඔබගේ HelaService ගිණුම සහ ඊට අදාළ සියලුම පුද්ගලික තොරතුරු ඕනෑම වේලාවක ස්ථිරවම මකා දැමිය හැක.

ක්‍රමය 1: ඇප් එක මඟින් මකා දැමීම (ක්ෂණිකව)
  1. HelaService ඇප් එක විවෘත කරන්න.
  2. Profile / ගිණුම අංශයට යන්න.
  3. පහළට ගොස් Delete Account (ගිණුම ස්ථිරවම මකන්න) තෝරන්න.
  4. තහවුරු කරන්න. ඔබගේ සියලු දත්ත පද්ධතියෙන් ඉවත් වනු ඇත.

මකා දමන දත්ත: ජාතික හැඳුනුම්පත් (NIC) ඡායාරූප, දුරකථන අංක, ලිපිනයන්, සජීවී ස්ථාන සහ ගිණුම් විස්තර ස්ථිරවම මකා දමනු ලැබේ.

தமிழ் (Tamil)

கணக்கு மற்றும் தனிப்பட்ட தரவை நீக்குதல்

உங்கள் HelaService கணக்கு மற்றும் தொடர்புடைய அனைத்து தனிப்பட்ட தரவுகளையும் நீங்கள் எந்த நேரத்திலும் நிரந்தரமாக நீக்கலாம்.

முறை 1: செயலி மூலம் நீக்குதல் (உடனடி)
  1. HelaService செயலியைத் திறக்கவும்.
  2. Profile / சுயவிவரம் பகுதிக்குச் செல்லவும்.
  3. கீழே சென்று Delete Account (கணக்கை நீக்கு) என்பதைத் தேர்ந்தெடுக்கவும்.
  4. உறுதிப்படுத்தவும். உங்கள் தரவு உடனடியாக அழிக்கப்படும்.

நீக்கப்படும் தரவு: தேசிய அடையாள அட்டை (NIC) படங்கள், தொலைபேசி எண்கள், இருப்பிடங்கள் மற்றும் சுயவிவரத் தகவல்கள் கணினியிலிருந்து நிரந்தரமாக அழிக்கப்படும்.
