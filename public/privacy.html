HelaService Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

HelaService ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information across our platform in Sri Lanka.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, and profile preferences.
  • Service Data: Job requests, service category selections, job locations, and communication records.
  • Artisan Verification: National Identity Card (NIC) details, photos, trade certifications, and location coordinates for verified providers.
  • Audit and Call Records: Timestamps of service dispatch, call attempt outcomes, and cash payment acknowledgments.

3. How We Use Information

We process your data to match customers with local service artisans, enable real-time location navigation for job fulfillment, verify artisan credentials for island-wide safety, and maintain transparent audit trails.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Users have the right to request full account deletion at any time directly through the app settings or via our API. Upon deletion, personal identification documents and account credentials are permanently purged.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to support@helaservice.lk.
